About Us

Your Wellness

Ce-Cy Pharmaceuticals is a licensed pharmaceutical importer and distributor based in Lagos. We specialize in procuring high-quality medical devices and consumer health products — from catheters and surgical disposables to advanced wound care and skin-adhesive solutions.

Our team combines regulatory, clinical and supply-chain expertise to ensure safe, timely and compliant delivery of products to hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and healthcare programmes across Nigeria.

Vision. Mission.

Ce-cypharm, we combine expertise, integrity, and compassion.

Our Vision

To be a leading, trusted supplier of safe and innovative medical devices and pharmaceutical products in West Africa — improving patient outcomes through quality, compliance and care.

Our Mission

To partner with healthcare providers to enhance access to essential medical consumables and wound care solution.

Core Values

We uphold the highest ethical standards in all our interactions and services, guided by integrity, compassion, and a deep sense of responsibility toward every life we touch.
