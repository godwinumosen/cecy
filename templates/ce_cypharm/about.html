About Us

Your Wellness

Ce-Cy Pharm Limited Pharmaceuticals is a licensed Pharmaceutical importer and distributor based in Lagos. We specialize in procuring high-quality medical devices and consumer health products — from catheters and surgical disposables to advanced wound care and skin-adhesive solutions.

Our team combines regulatory, clinical and supply-chain expertise to ensure safe, timely and compliant delivery of products to hospitals, clinics, Pharmacies and healthcare programmes across Nigeria.
